How long does a typical project take?

−

Most projects take between two to six weeks depending on scope, research depth, and revision rounds. Clear timelines are shared before we begin.

What does your design process look like?

+

I start by understanding your goals and users, then move through research, wireframing, visual design, prototyping, and refinement to deliver a thoughtful final experience.

Do you offer revisions?

+

Yes. Feedback is an important part of the process. I work collaboratively with clients to refine the design and ensure the final result aligns with the project goals.

Do you provide post-launch support?

+

Yes. I can provide design support, improvements, and guidance after delivery to help your product continue evolving.
