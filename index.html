Featured Projects

HR Management Dashboard

A complete redesign of workforce management for a growing tech company. Turning HR chaos into clarity through thoughtful information architecture and human-centered design.

The Problem

HR teams were drowning in fragmented tools—spreadsheets, emails, and legacy software that didn't talk to each other. Employee data was scattered, onboarding took weeks, and managers lacked visibility into team performance. The existing system was costing time, money, and talent.

The Process

Started with stakeholder interviews across HR, management, and employees to map pain points. Built user personas and journey maps, then moved into rapid wireframing and iterative prototyping. Weekly testing sessions with actual users kept us honest and on track.

The Solution

Designed a unified dashboard that centralizes employee data, automates onboarding workflows, and gives managers real-time insights. Clean, modular interface with role-based views—HR sees everything, managers see their teams, employees see their growth.

The Result

Reduced onboarding time by 60%, cut HR admin work by 40%, and improved employee satisfaction scores. The design system I built is now being rolled out across the company's entire product suite.

HimClothiers E-Commerce

HimClothiers — Premium Menswear E-Commerce

A luxury fashion e-commerce experience designed for the modern Nigerian man. Blending contemporary aesthetics with seamless shopping flows to elevate a local brand to international standards.

The Problem

HimClothiers had exceptional products but a digital presence that didn't match their quality. The existing site suffered from high bounce rates (68%), abandoned carts (74%), and a checkout process that frustrated mobile users. Customers couldn't visualize fit, fabric quality, or styling options—critical barriers in online fashion retail.

The Process

Conducted competitive analysis of luxury fashion retailers (Mr Porter, SSENSE) and local Nigerian e-commerce patterns. Interviewed 20+ target customers to understand shopping behaviors. Mapped the entire purchase journey from discovery to unboxing, identifying friction points at size selection, payment trust, and delivery expectations.

The Solution

Designed a mobile-first experience with immersive product photography, detailed size guides with body-type recommendations, and a streamlined 3-step checkout. Added a "Style Guide" feature showing complete looks, AR fabric texture preview, and multiple payment options including local methods (Paystack, bank transfer) alongside international cards.

The Result

Bounce rate dropped to 34%, cart abandonment reduced to 41%, and mobile conversion increased by 156% within three months. Average order value grew 28% through the style guide upsells. The brand successfully launched international shipping to the UK and US based on the new platform's credibility.

GOALIO Livescore App

GOALIO — Football Livescore & Stats App

A real-time football livescore application designed for passionate fans who demand instant updates, deep statistics, and personalized match tracking. Bringing the stadium experience to the palm of your hand.

The Problem

Football fans were frustrated with existing livescore apps that were either too cluttered with ads, too slow with updates, or missing crucial features like detailed player stats and push notifications for specific events (goals, red cards, substitutions). Most apps treated all users the same, ignoring the fact that a Manchester United fan in Lagos has different needs than a casual viewer.

The Process

Shadowed 15 football fans during match days to understand their behaviors—when they check scores, what triggers notifications, how they share moments with friends. Analyzed top competitors (FlashScore, SofaScore, FotMob) to identify gaps in the Nigerian market. Conducted card sorting exercises to organize 50+ data points per match into intuitive hierarchies.

The Solution

Built a lightning-fast mobile experience with sub-second score updates, customizable notification triggers, and a "Match Pulse" feature showing momentum shifts through visual heatmaps. Designed a team-centric home screen that surfaces your club's news first, plus a social layer for celebrating goals with friends via quick-share graphics and reaction emojis.

The Result

Beta testing with 500 users showed 89% daily active retention during Premier League weekends. Average session time hit 12 minutes—3x industry standard. Push notification open rates reached 67% (vs. 15% average) due to hyper-relevant timing. Currently in closed beta with 2,000+ waitlist signups ahead of public launch.
